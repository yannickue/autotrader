## Decisions that follow
* No existing implementation is replaced; `demo.structure.confirmed_swings` stays the single swing source of truth.
* Two tests become Definition-of-Done for every new feature group: RECURSIVE/WARMUP INVARIANCE and HTF CLOSED-BAR ALIGNMENT (besides prefix invariance).
* One deferred shadow hypothesis is registered: volatility-normalised confirmed swings (ZigZag-inspired). It is not built now.
* No volume-profile/POC/value-area features and no exchange-volume interpretation of MT5 tick counts.
* The scout's items 5 (IGNORE) and 7 (SHADOW_CHALLENGER) were overruled by the lead as above; item 1's remark that our approach is 'unsuitable for live trading' is incorrect (our swings are explicitly causal with the n-bar confirmation delay).
